## The look you're after
The reference photos feel warm, rich and a little moody. Most are tall frames, often with leaves or an arch around the edges, and a small person for scale. The biggest change is to shoot more upright, expose a touch darker, and look for stronger colour instead of pale, hazy light.

## What holds your photos back
Many of your photos feel light, pale and a bit washed out, especially on hazy days, and most are wide frames with a lot of open sky. That makes them airy and calm, but less rich and moody than the references. (photos: RXM02820.JPG, IMG_8748.JPG, DSC00695.JPG, IMG_1062.JPG)
